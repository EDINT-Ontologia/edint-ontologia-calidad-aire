Air Quality: Description back to ToC

Ontology for representing air quality data. It reuses the Environmental sensors pattern to represent stations, sensors and air quality observations (NO2, O3, PM10, etc.). It reuses the pattern of the EDINT Environmental Sensors Ontology —sensor platform (edintmed:SensorPlatform), environmental sensors (edintmed:EnvironmentalSensor) and environmental observations (edintmed:EnvironmentalObservation)— and specialises the air quality domain through SKOS schemes: the sensor type with SensorTypes (for example, AirQualitySensor), the observed properties with SensorVariables (for example, NO2Concentration, O3Concentration or PM10Concentration) and the feature of interest with FeaturesOfInterest (for example, Air or ParticulateMatter).

Figure 1 shows an example of use of the Environmental Sensors Ontology for Air Quality.

This example illustrates the use of the Environmental Sensor Ontology for the air quality domain. It features an air quality station with the identifier "AQ_001," which is an instance of edintmed:SensorPlatform. This station includes an edintmed:environmentalSensor with the identifier "AQS_001," which is sosa:hostedBy the station. Its type within the kos:SensorTypes SKOS taxonomy is "AirQualitySensor." Note that the environmental sensor ontology defines the station as a subclass of geosparql:Feature, allowing for the representation of its geospatial data. The sensor's schema:manufacturer is the company "AirTech." The figure depicts an NO2 (nitrogen dioxide) observation, which is an instance of edintmed:EnvironmentalObservation. Specifically, the sosa:observedProperty property links the observation to an edintmed:EnvironmentalProperty—"NO2Concentration"—a value belonging to the kos:EnvironmentalProperty SKOS taxonomy. The observation has a sosa:hasFeatureOfInterest—in this case, "Air"—which belongs to the kos:FeaturesOfInterest SKOS taxonomy. The observation has a sosa:hasSimpleResult of "26.0" in the unit "MicroGM-Per-M3" (an instance of unit:Unit), representing the nitrogen dioxide concentration per cubic meter.
